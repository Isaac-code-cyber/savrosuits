The Atelier

Where craft
meets character

Savro Suits was born from a simple conviction: that a man deserves a suit built entirely around him — not the other way around. Founded in Israel by a tailor who spent years studying the old traditions of European menswear, Savro is a house that refuses to compromise on the fundamentals of its craft.

Every commission begins with a conversation. We want to understand who you are, how you move through the world, and what you need the suit to do for you. Every pattern is drawn to your measurements. Every seam is placed by hand. Every lining is chosen for its feel as much as its beauty.

Savro is not fast fashion. It is the belief that something worth wearing is worth taking time over — and that when it is finally ready, it will fit you like nothing you have ever put on before.

How it works

The Bespoke Journey

From your first visit to the final fitting, every step is guided by our tailors — unhurried and entirely personal.

01

Consultation

We meet to understand your lifestyle, occasions, and aesthetic. Every detail — shoulder line, button stance, trouser break — is discussed and decided by you.

02

Cloth Selection

Browse over 800 fabrics — English wools, Italian linens, silk blends — from mills that have clothed generations of well-dressed men.

03

Pattern & Craft

A pattern is drafted exclusively to your measurements. Your suit is then cut, sewn, and hand-finished in our atelier — never outsourced.

04

Final Fitting

We refine until perfection. When the suit leaves Savro, it fits you precisely. Included alterations are yours for life.
